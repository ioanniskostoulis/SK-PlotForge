## Original work

The TypeScript application, the interface, the command console, the file readers (TIFF, PNG, JPEG, XLSX and delimited text), the image steps, thresholds and measurements, the plots and their layout, and the fits are new code. Image steps, threshold methods and measurements follow the definitions of ImageJ and of the papers named at the head of each function (`source/engine/threshold.ts`, `filters.ts`, `binary.ts`, `measure.ts`); no ImageJ source is copied. The default look of plots follows the owner's report charts made in Excel.
